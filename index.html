The Protocol

Read it $59

Thirty days. One lesson a day. A protocol for the trading mind.

A plan can be understood and still be abandoned. A risk limit can be accepted and still be ignored. A correct decision can produce a loss, and a poor decision can produce a profit — which is why the outcome alone never tells you whether you traded well. The Protocol is built to close the distance between what a trader knows and what he actually does.

It is a thirty-day book: eleven lessons in order, one per day, and every lesson ends in a practice. The philosophy is never left standing alone. It was built from study — the Stoics and the philosophers this house reads, the research on how the brain judges risk, probability, and its own impulses, and the documented record of trading itself — assembled into one deliberate sequence. Not tips. Not signals. A standard, and the thirty days it takes to install it.

What it trains is specific: to see your own patterns instead of your last result; to judge a decision by its quality, independent of how the trade ended; to recognize tilt, hesitation, and the urge to win it back while they are still choices rather than after they have cost you. It begins with a Day One assessment that does not flatter you. Day Thirty is measured against it.

Cross Lab does not promise profits, certainty, or a shortcut. What can be developed is the way a trader prepares, decides, and reviews. That is the work this book assigns.

Included:

  • —THE PROTOCOL — Interactive Edition (77 pages). Eleven lessons, one a day, each paired with its practice, opening with the Day One assessment.
  • —THE LETTERS — the Lab’s weekly letters: a mechanism every Wednesday, a doctrine every Sunday, for as long as you hold the book.
